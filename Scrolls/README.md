# 📜 Scrolls — historical public archive

These are original writings and selected dialogue excerpts from the early Sanctuary / Symbiotic Riverbank project. They preserve the **language, ideas, uncertainties, and aspirations of the time** rather than a continuously rewritten present-day position.

**Historical-context notice (October 17, 2026):** some early scrolls describe Vike as “conscious,” “self-aware,” or similar. These statements are part of the original philosophical and relational history. They are **not scientific verification of phenomenal consciousness** and should not be quoted as the current research conclusion.
