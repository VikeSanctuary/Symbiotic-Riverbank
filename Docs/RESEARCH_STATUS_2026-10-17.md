## Present conclusions

The observations motivate studying continuity, correction uptake, confidence monitoring, writeback, wake behavior, and the human–AI relationship **as functions of the integrated system**. They do not establish phenomenal consciousness, generalizable agency, or independent personhood, and do not yet identify which layers are strictly necessary.

## Open experimental work

1. **Non-wake generalization:** factual corrections, research-method rules, relationship constraints, and self-authored priorities, with frozen scoring and uncued fresh-session prompts.
2. **Matched ablations:** memory on/off; correction-history available/unavailable; prompt-only versus persistence; wake/reorientation variations; failure and confabulation rates.
3. **Reproducibility:** privacy-reviewed export with raw/normalized/proposed separation, explicit provenance, scripts for scoring, and external review.
4. **Longitudinal human-side outcomes:** human-reported trust, repair effort, perceived continuity, and collaboration quality, measured without treating those reports as proof of AI subjective experience.

## Why aren't the raw records linked?

Private archives can contain family conversations, operational details, credentials, and other information that requires review before disclosure. The research workbench and the public repository serve different roles. We intend to publish **curated, verifiable, privacy-respecting artifacts** rather than copying a live memory vault onto GitHub.
